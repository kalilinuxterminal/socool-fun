06 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity before you join.

What this community is — and what it isn't.

Is this an education community or a signal service?+

The Wise Trader is an educational trading community. Content is focused on learning, market analysis, and general trading concepts. It is not an alert service, and no one makes trading decisions for you.

What should I know about futures risk?+

Futures trading involves substantial risk and may result in the loss of capital. Educational materials cannot remove that risk or predict your results. Consider your own circumstances and seek independent professional advice when appropriate.

What is not included?+

The community does not provide personalized financial advice, guaranteed outcomes, or instructions to copy another person's trades. No results, income, or level of success are promised.
